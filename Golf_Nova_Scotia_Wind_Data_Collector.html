Wind Data Collector

Pick a province, then a golf course and one or more years. Each year covers the province’s handicap active season, and each day runs from a set time after sunrise to a set time before sunset at the course. The tool finds the closest Environment Canada wind station within 100 km, pulls its hourly readings, and averages wind speed by day, week (Monday to Sunday), month, year and hour of day.

This page looks like it is open in a preview window. You can search courses and stations here, but getting wind data needs the page opened in Safari or Chrome.

Nearest wind stations

⛳ Course   Selected station   Other station   No data for your years. Click a station on the map to select or unselect it.

Every wind station within 100 km is listed, closest first; the five nearest show here, so scroll or filter to see the rest. The closest station with hourly data covering your years is ticked. Tick more to include them, for example an airport or one on the same coast; their readings are combined. Some airports have two records for the same site. The map above shows the course and every station; zoom out to see them all. Use the Satellite link to see a station’s surroundings in Google Maps; station positions are given to about 10 m.
