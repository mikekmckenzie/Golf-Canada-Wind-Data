Golf Nova Scotia
Wind Data Collector

Pick a province, then a golf course and a date range. The tool finds the closest Environment Canada wind station within 100 km, pulls its hourly readings, and averages wind speed by day, week (Monday to Sunday), month and hour of day.

This page looks like it is open in a preview window. You can search courses and stations here, but getting wind data needs the page opened in Safari or Chrome.

Nearest wind stations

Every wind station within 100 km is listed, closest first; scroll or filter to find one. The closest station with hourly data covering your dates is selected. Choose another if it suits the course better, for example an airport or one on the same coast. Some airports have two records for the same site.

Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada hourly climate data (api.weather.gc.ca). Speeds are km/h. Direction is where the wind blows from. Times are local clock time at the course. Recent readings are preliminary. A station can be many kilometres from the course and sit in different terrain, so treat the numbers as a guide to the area and not a measurement at the course. Course locations come from OpenStreetMap; those marked "town location" are placed at the club's town.
